# Indian citation forms

Forms, not an exhaustive style guide. Where a court has changed its format, trust the court's own portal over this file.

## Neutral citations (court-issued, reporter-independent)

A neutral citation identifies the judgment itself — court, year, serial number — and is the same whichever reporter publishes it. It is the fastest route to the official copy and should lead the citation string.

| Court | Pattern | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supreme Court of India | `YYYY INSC NNN` | `2024 INSC 205` | Introduced 2023 for all judgments; assigned retrospectively to earlier judgments on eSCR (pre-2023 INSC numbers exist — verify them on eSCR, they were back-filled). Serial numbers run to roughly 1,000–1,300 a year; a number in the thousands for a single year is suspect. |
| High Courts (general) | `YYYY:CODE:NNNNN` with optional bench suffix | `2024:DHC:1234`, `2024:DHC:1234-DB` | Colon-separated. Codes and suffixes differ by court; a `-DB` suffix marks a Division Bench in some courts. |
| Delhi | `YYYY:DHC:NNNN[-DB]` | `2023:DHC:4567-DB` | |
| Bombay | `YYYY:BHC-AS:NNNNN`, `BHC-OS`, `BHC-NAG`, `BHC-AUG`, `BHC-GOA` | `2024:BHC-AS:12345` | AS = appellate side, OS = original side, then benches (Nagpur, Aurangabad, Goa); some orders carry `-DB`. |
| Kerala | `YYYY:KER:NNNNN` | `2024:KER:12345` | |
| Madras | `YYYY:MHC:NNNN` | `2024:MHC:1234` | |
| Allahabad | `YYYY:AHC:NNNNNN`, `AHC-LKO` | `2024:AHC:123456`, `2024:AHC-LKO:12345` | Lucknow bench suffix. |
| Karnataka | `YYYY:KHC:NNNNN`, `KHC-D`, `KHC-K` | `2024:KHC:12345` | Dharwad and Kalaburagi benches. |
| Punjab & Haryana | `YYYY:PHHC:NNNNNN` | `2024:PHHC:123456` | |
| Gujarat | `YYYY:GUJHC:NNNNN` | `2024:GUJHC:12345` | |
| Madhya Pradesh | `YYYY:MPHC-IND:NNNNN`, `MPHC-JBP`, `MPHC-GWL` | `2024:MPHC-JBP:12345` | Indore, Jabalpur, Gwalior. |
| Rajasthan | `YYYY:RJ-JD:NNNNN`, `RJ-JP` | `2024:RJ-JP:12345` | Jodhpur, Jaipur. |
| Calcutta | `YYYY:CHC-AS:NNNNN`, `CHC-OS` | `2024:CHC-AS:1234` | Appellate / original side. |
| Himachal Pradesh | `YYYY:HHC:NNNN` | `2024:HHC:1234` | |

Other High Courts use the same colon pattern with their own code (Orissa, Patna, Telangana, Andhra, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand, Gauhati, Manipur, Meghalaya, Tripura, Sikkim, J&K and Ladakh). If the code is not in the table, the form is still plausible — check the court's portal; do not reject it on the code alone.

## Reporter citations

| Reporter | Pattern | Example | Sanity limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supreme Court Cases (SCC) | `(YYYY) V SCC P` | `(2010) 11 SCC 441` | From 1969. Volumes per year have grown: single digits in early years, up to the mid-teens recently; a volume above ~20 is suspect. Supplementary volumes: `(1995) Supp (2) SCC 100`. Topical series: `(2019) 1 SCC (Cri) 123`, `SCC (Civ)`, `SCC (L&S)`. |
| Supreme Court Reports (SCR, official) | `[YYYY] V SCR P` or `(YYYY) V SCR P`; newer `YYYY (V) SCR P` / digital `[YYYY] V S.C.R. P` | `[1973] Supp SCR 1`, `2023 (1) SCR 1` | Official reporter; eSCR is its digital form. |
| All India Reporter (AIR) | `AIR YYYY SC P` / `AIR YYYY Del P` | `AIR 2019 SC 1234` | From 1914. Page numbers can run to five digits in recent years. Newer AIR uses `AIR 2023 SUPREME COURT 1234` on its own site. |
| SCALE | `YYYY (V) SCALE P` | `2019 (3) SCALE 123` | |
| Judgments Today (JT) | `JT YYYY (V) SC P` | `JT 2019 (3) SC 123` | |
| SCC OnLine | `YYYY SCC OnLine SC P` / `SCC OnLine Del P` / `SCC OnLine Bom P` | `2020 SCC OnLine SC 889` | Database citation for unreported or pre-print judgments; court abbreviation follows OnLine. |
| Manupatra | `MANU/SC/NNNN/YYYY`, `MANU/DE/NNNN/YYYY` | `MANU/SC/0123/2019` | Database identifier, not a reporter. |
| Criminal Law Journal | `YYYY Cri LJ P` | `2019 Cri LJ 1234` | |
| Older official reports | `(YYYY) ILR V Cal P`, `AIR 1950 FC P`, `(1949) 51 Bom LR P` | | Pre-1950 Federal Court and Privy Council: `AIR 1945 PC 48`; `(1944) 71 IA 1`. |

## Statutes

- Full first mention: `section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881`; thereafter `s.138 NI Act` or `s.138`.
- Sub-sections and clauses: `s.142(1)(b)`, `s.318(4) BNS`, `Article 20(1) of the Constitution`.
- The new criminal codes: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS); Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS); Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA) — in force 1 July 2024. IPC/CrPC/IEA references are correct only for matters governed by the old codes under the transition rules.
- Rules and regulations: `Rule 7, DPDP Rules, 2025`; `Regulation 30, SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015`.
- Notifications: `S.O. 850(E) dated 23 February 2024` (Gazette number and date).

## Conventions

- **Neutral vs reporter**: `2024 INSC 205` identifies the judgment; `(2024) 4 SCC 419` identifies where it is printed. Give the neutral citation first, then one reporter: `Rakesh Ranjan Shrivastava v. State of Jharkhand, 2024 INSC 205 : (2024) 4 SCC 419`. Parallel citations are joined by ` : ` or `; `.
- **(supra)**: after the first full citation, `Rangappa (supra)` or `Rangappa (supra), para 26` refers back; every `(supra)` must have a full citation earlier in the same document — the script flags `(supra)` with no earlier match.
- **Pinpoints**: `para 12` for paragraphs, `at p. 450` for pages. SCC numbers paragraphs itself; eSCR/official copies have their own numbering — say which copy the pinpoint follows.
- **Case names**: `v.` not `vs.`; parties as in the official copy (`State of Maharashtra`, `Union of India`, `State (NCT of Delhi)`); criminal appeals are often `X v. State of Y`.
- **Bench and date** when relevant to weight: `(Constitution Bench)`, `(3-judge bench)`, `decided on 14 March 2024`.
