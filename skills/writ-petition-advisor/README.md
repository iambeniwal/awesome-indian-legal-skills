# Writ Petition Advisor

Answers the question practitioners get asked constantly — "can we go to the High Court on this?" — with the analysis a senior would run: is there a legal right and a duty, is the respondent "State" or performing a public duty, which Article (32 / 226 / 227) and which writ, does an alternative remedy shut the door (and does an exception open it), is the delay fatal, where does the cause of action place the petition, and what relief is realistic. Then it builds the skeleton: synopsis and list of dates, lettered grounds tied to decisions, prayer and interim prayer — or tells you the statutory route and its deadline when a writ is the wrong tool.

For advocates and litigants.

## Install

```bash
claude plugin install writ-petition-advisor@awesome-indian-legal-skills
```

Or copy this folder into your agent's skills directory.

## What it needs from you

- The impugned act or inaction, with dates and the order itself
- The right asserted and who the respondent is (and the control/funding/function facts if it is not obviously Government)
- What you want the court to do; any statutory appeal available and its deadline
- Earlier representations and any earlier litigation on the same cause

## What you get

- Maintainability memo and a filled decision table
- Article 12 analysis where needed
- Draft synopsis/list of dates, lettered grounds, prayer and interim prayer
- Documents list and the High Court-specific items to verify (rules, Bench, court fee, PIL rules)
- When a writ does not lie: the statutory route, forum and deadline

## Example prompts

- "The municipal corporation sealed our shop without notice under a 2019 order we never received. Can we file a writ in Delhi High Court, or is there an appeal?"
- "Private university terminated a professor mid-contract. Does a writ lie?"
- "Our GST registration was cancelled; the appeal period expired last month. Writ?"

## Law covered

- Constitution of India, Arts. 12, 32, 136, 226, 227, 323A/B; Supreme Court Rules, 2013 (Orders XXI, XXXVIII)
- *Pradeep Kumar Biswas*, *Ajay Hasia*, *Zee Telefilms*, *Andi Mukta*, *Janet Jeyapaul*, *St. Mary's Education Society*; *Whirlpool*, *Harbanslal Sahnia*, *Radha Krishan Industries*, *Godrej Sara Lee*, *Glaxo Smith Kline*, *PHR Invent* (2024 INSC 297); *L. Chandra Kumar*; *Kusum Ingots*; *Radhey Shyam v. Chhabi Nath*; *Balwant Singh Chaufal* — see [references/case-law.md](references/case-law.md)

## Limits

- Every High Court's rules, Bench allocation and court fee differ; the skill lists what to verify rather than guessing.
- Criminal quashing (BNSS s.528) and statutory appeals are separate routes; the skill routes to them.
- It does not predict listing or outcomes.
