# Glossary: Hindi / Hinglish ↔ English, with the statutory term

Use the statutory term exactly, give the English in brackets the first time, and keep the user's script. Section numbers are the post-1 July 2024 codes (BNS / BNSS / BSA) unless noted; verify on India Code before stating more than the number.

## Words to avoid translating loosely

- *Zamanat* is bail, never "release" or "acquittal" (*bari hona*).
- *Shikayat* to a Magistrate (s.223 BNSS) is not an FIR (s.173 BNSS); both exist and lead to different routes.
- *Kabza* means both lawful possession and unlawful encroachment — ask which before routing.
- *Notice* from police (s.35(3) BNSS) is not a summons from a court, and not a legal notice from a lawyer; each has a different consequence for ignoring it.
- *Case* may mean an FIR, a civil suit, a consumer complaint or a RERA complaint — ask for the forum and the number.
