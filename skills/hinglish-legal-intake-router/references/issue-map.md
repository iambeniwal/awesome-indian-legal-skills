# Issue map: what people say → what it is → where it goes

Match on meaning, not exact words; users misspell, mix scripts and use regional variants ("cheque bounce", "check bounce", "chek wapas aa gaya"). One primary route, at most one secondary. "No skill yet" rows must be answered honestly — name the official route and legal aid.

Deadline flags: **D-days** = act within days; **D-weeks** = within weeks; **D-years** = long but real; **—** = none that bites now. The receiving skill computes the real dates.

## Phrases that signal "no lawyer, no money"

"vakil ka paisa nahi hai", "garib aadmi hoon", "mazdoor", "ghar se nikaal diya", "akeli hoon", "student hoon", "pension" — add the legal-aid line: free legal aid under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 via NALSA/DLSA (15100), Tele-Law at the nearest CSC, Nyaya Bandhu. Verify numbers are current.

## Phrases that signal urgency over routing

"abhi utha ke le gaye", "maar rahe hain", "jaan ka khatra", "bachche ko", "aaj raat", "kal subah pesh karenge", "paisa abhi kat raha hai" — emergency line first (112 / 181 / 1098 / 1930), then the route.
