# Labour Codes — obligations checklist (audit order)

Mark each item Met / Partly / Not met / N/A with the evidence seen, and note whether the Central Rules or the State's rules govern it. Thresholds marked (verify) are set by Rules or notification and may differ by State.

## 1. Scope and headcount

- [ ] Establishment type fixed (factory / shop or commercial / construction / mine / plantation / transport / gig platform / contractor) and the appropriate Government identified (Central or State).
- [ ] Headcount computed the Code's way: all employees, including contract workers for most thresholds; workers vs non-workers (supervisory above the notified wage line, managerial/administrative) separated — IR Code applies to "workers".
- [ ] Thresholds crossed in the last 12 months listed: 10 (OSH registration; ESIC), 20 (PF; works committee at 100; GRC at 20), 50 (contract-labour licence — verify), 300 (standing orders; Chapter X permission — or the State's figure).

## 2. Registrations and licences

- [ ] Single registration under OSH Code s.3 (replaces factory/contract-labour/BOCW/ISMW registrations) obtained or migrated; registration number displayed.
- [ ] Factory licence where s.2(w) applies (10 with power / 20 without).
- [ ] Contract-labour licence for contractors above the threshold; principal-employer registration; contractor's licence verified before engagement.
- [ ] EPFO and ESIC codes; ESIC coverage checked pan-India (the "implemented area" limitation has gone).
- [ ] State Shops & Establishments registration (still required).
- [ ] Electronic registers and returns maintained in the prescribed forms; unified annual return filed.

## 3. Wages

- [ ] Pay structure reviewed under s.2(y): basic + DA + retaining allowance ≥ 50% of total remuneration, or the excess added back; the "wages" figure documented for every employee.
- [ ] Minimum wages per the State schedule (and not below the floor wage) for each category and zone; revised when the schedule is.
- [ ] Monthly wages paid by the 7th of the following month; weekly/fortnightly per s.17; full and final settlement within two working days of exit (verify the Rules' period).
- [ ] Deductions only those permitted, total ≤ 50% of wages (s.18).
- [ ] Equal remuneration for the same or similar work across genders; no discrimination in recruitment (s.3).
- [ ] Bonus: eligibility ceiling and calculation base per the latest notification (verify); minimum 8.33%, maximum 20% (ss.26–27); payable within eight months of the accounting year's close; set-on/set-off register.
- [ ] Wage slips, electronic or physical, issued.

## 4. Hours, leave, conditions (OSH Code + State S&E Act)

- [ ] Appointment letter issued to every employee in the prescribed form (s.6) — including existing staff.
- [ ] Daily hours within the baseline (s.25) and any extended day only within the Rules' limits and the weekly cap; spread-over observed; overtime at twice the wage rate with consent (s.26); overtime register.
- [ ] Weekly day of rest; compensatory off where worked.
- [ ] Leave with wages accrued and carried forward per s.32 and the Rules; encashment on exit.
- [ ] Women employed before 6 a.m./after 7 p.m. only with written consent and the prescribed safety, transport and facilities (s.43).
- [ ] Annual health examination for notified classes of workers (s.20).
- [ ] Welfare facilities per headcount: canteen, crèche, first aid, restrooms, ambulance room (verify thresholds in the Rules); safety committee and safety officer where required (Chapter V).
- [ ] Inter-State migrant workers: registration, journey allowance, portability (Chapter XII).

## 5. Social security (CSS Code)

- [ ] PF: contributions on "wages" (post-50% rule) for every eligible employee; international workers handled; nominations.
- [ ] ESIC: contributions for employees under the wage ceiling (verify current ceiling); hazardous establishments covered regardless of size.
- [ ] Gratuity: policy and accrual on the Code's wage base; five-year qualifying service; pro-rata for fixed-term employees after one year; payable within 30 days of it becoming due, with interest thereafter; nominations; insurance where required (verify).
- [ ] Maternity: 26 weeks (12 for the third child onwards), crèche where 50+ employees, no dismissal during maternity absence (Chapter VI).
- [ ] Employees' compensation for injury/death where ESIC does not apply (Chapter VII).
- [ ] Gig/platform workers: registration on the e-Shram portal; aggregator contribution under s.114 when operational (verify).

## 6. Industrial relations (IR Code)

- [ ] Standing orders: Model Standing Orders 2026 adopted or own orders certified where 300+ workers (s.28); displayed.
- [ ] Fixed-term employment contracts: equal wages/benefits, pro-rata gratuity, no conversion by default (s.2(l)).
- [ ] Grievance redressal committee constituted (20+ workers; up to ten members; equal worker representation; women proportionate) — s.4.
- [ ] Works committee where 100+ workers (s.3).
- [ ] Negotiating union / council recognised at 51% / proportional (s.14).
- [ ] Lay-off, retrenchment, closure: notice and compensation under ss.65–72; **prior permission** under Chapter X where the threshold applies; re-skilling fund contribution (s.83) within 45 days of retrenchment (verify).
- [ ] Strike/lock-out: 14-day notice regime understood; no action during conciliation/pending proceedings (s.62).
- [ ] Disciplinary process: charge-sheet, inquiry per standing orders and natural justice, reasoned order; suspension allowance.

## 7. Governance

- [ ] Compliance calendar (returns, contributions, bonus, registers) with owners.
- [ ] Inspector-cum-facilitator readiness: web-based inspection, records retrievable.
- [ ] Compounding policy for first offences understood (each Code has a compounding section).
- [ ] Old-regime matters (pre-21 Nov 2025 accruals, pending ID Act references) tracked separately.
