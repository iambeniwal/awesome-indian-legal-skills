## What was repealed

The Codes subsume **29 Central labour laws**, including: Payment of Wages Act 1936, Minimum Wages Act 1948, Payment of Bonus Act 1965, Equal Remuneration Act 1976 (→ Wages); Trade Unions Act 1926, Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act 1946, Industrial Disputes Act 1947 (→ IR); EPF & MP Act 1952, ESI Act 1948, Maternity Benefit Act 1961, Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, Employees' Compensation Act 1923, Building and Other Construction Workers Acts, Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act 2008 (→ Social Security); Factories Act 1948, Contract Labour (R&A) Act 1970, Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act 1979, Mines Act 1952, Plantations Labour Act, Motor Transport Workers Act, Working Journalists Acts, Cine-Workers Acts, Dock Workers Act, Beedi and Cigar Workers Act (→ OSH). State Shops and Establishments Acts are **not** repealed.

## What survives

- **Transitional savings**: each Code saves rules, notifications, schemes, standing orders, registrations and licences made under the repealed Acts until superseded. Where a State has not notified rules under a Code, the old State rules (e.g. its Minimum Wages Rules, Factories Rules, Contract Labour Rules) continue to apply mutatis mutandis.
- **Accrued rights**: gratuity, wages, bonus and compensation that accrued before 21 November 2025 are computed under the repealed Act; pending proceedings continue under it.
- **State Shops & Establishments Acts** continue to govern hours, leave and holidays for commercial establishments alongside the OSH Code; where they conflict, read the OSH Code's overriding provisions and the State's position.
- **Minimum wage schedules**: existing State notifications continue until revised under the Code's floor-wage mechanism.

## Thresholds the States can change

| Matter | Code default | Who can vary |
|---|---|---|
| Prior permission for lay-off, retrenchment, closure (IR Code Chapter X) | 300 workers | Appropriate Government, by notification (s.77(1)) |
| Standing orders | 300 workers (s.28) | Appropriate Government |
| Daily hours / spread-over / overtime limits | 8 hours baseline (OSH s.25) | Rules by the appropriate Government |
| Contract-labour licensing | 50 workers (verify) | Rules |
| Registration under OSH Code | 10 workers (verify) | Rules |

## Key sections (for citation)

- Wages: s.2(y) definition and the 50% rule; s.3 equal remuneration; ss.6–9 minimum and floor wage; s.17 time of payment; s.18 deductions; ss.26–41 bonus; s.45 claims and the 3-year limitation (s.45(6)); s.54 compounding.
- IR Code: s.2(l) fixed-term; s.4 grievance redressal committee; s.14 negotiating union/council; s.28 standing orders; ss.44–61 tribunals; s.62 strike notice; ss.65–72 lay-off/retrenchment/closure (general); ss.77–81 Chapter X permission; s.83 re-skilling fund; s.89 compounding.
- Social Security: Chapter III (PF), Chapter IV (ESIC), Chapter V (gratuity — s.53), Chapter VI (maternity), Chapter VII (employees' compensation), Chapter IX (unorganised/gig/platform — s.114 aggregator contribution); s.138 compounding.
- OSH: s.3 registration; s.6 appointment letter; s.20 health check; ss.25–33 hours, overtime, leave, weekly off; s.43 women; Chapter XI contract labour; Chapter XII inter-State migrant workers; s.97 compounding.
