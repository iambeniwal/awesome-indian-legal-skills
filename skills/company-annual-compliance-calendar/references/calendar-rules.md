## Additional fees vs penalties

- **Additional fee** (s.403; Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules): ₹100 per day for every day of delay on s.92 (MGT-7/7A) and s.137 (AOC-4) filings, with no cap; other forms carry multiplier slabs (2×, 4×, 6×, 10×, 12× of the normal fee by delay band). The form will not be accepted without it. It is not a fine.
- **Adjudication penalty** (ss.92(5), 137(3), 99, 173(4), 118(11), etc.): imposed by the ROC/adjudicating officer after notice, on the company and officers in default; small companies, OPCs, startups and producer companies pay half, capped (s.446B). Separate from, and in addition to, the additional fee.
- **DIN deactivation** for a missed DIR-3 KYC is immediate and blocks every filing by that director until ₹5,000 is paid.

| Board meetings | 4, gap ≤120 d | 2, one per half, ≥90 d apart | 2 (or 1 if sole director; s.173(5)) | 1 per six months | none |
| AGM | yes | yes | **no** | yes | none |
| Annual return | MGT-7 | MGT-7A | MGT-7A | MGT-7 | Form 11 |

| Secretarial standards | yes | yes | yes | yes | no |
| CSR | by thresholds | by thresholds | by thresholds | by thresholds (and often a CSR recipient) | no |
| Audit | yes | yes | yes | yes | thresholds |

**Small company** (s.2(85) + Rule 2(1)(t)): paid-up capital ≤₹4 crore and turnover ≤₹40 crore per G.S.R. 700(E) (15 Sept 2022); a further increase has been reported — verify before relying. Not a holding/subsidiary, Section 8 company, or a company governed by a special Act.

## Structural routes

- **Dormant company** (s.455): no significant accounting transaction; apply in MSC-1; minimal filings (MSC-3 annually); can hold for five years.
- **Strike-off** (s.248; STK-2): voluntary after extinguishing liabilities; blocked if INC-20A, AOC-4 or MGT-7 are pending for the relevant years or if the company changed its name/registered office or disposed of property in the last three months.
- **Conversion**: OPC to private/public (voluntary; mandatory conversion thresholds removed from 1 April 2021 — verify); private to LLP and back (Rules); small company status is tested each year on the thresholds.

## Tax and GST pointers (verify — these change and now sit under the Income-tax Act, 2025)

- ITR: 31 July (no audit) / 31 October (audit cases) under the 1961 Act's calendar; confirm the 2025 Act's dates for tax year 2026-27 onwards. Tax audit report before the ITR. Advance tax 15 June / 15 September / 15 December / 15 March. TDS returns quarterly (31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 May); Form 16/16A issue dates.
- GST: GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B monthly (or QRMP), GSTR-9 annual return and GSTR-9C reconciliation by 31 December; e-invoicing thresholds.
- State: professional-tax returns, Shops & Establishment renewals, labour-code registers under the Labour Codes in force since 21 November 2025 (Central Rules 8 May 2026).
