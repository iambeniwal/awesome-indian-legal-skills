# Transition rules: 1961 Act → 2025 Act

The Income-tax Act, 2025 (reported as Act No. 30 of 2025, assented 21 August 2025 — verify the Act number on India Code) came into force on **1 April 2026** and repealed the Income-tax Act, 1961 by **s.536(1)**. The savings clauses are in **s.536(2)** (a long list of lettered clauses; commentators count 22), supplemented by s.6 of the General Clauses Act, 1897 for anything the list misses. The Income-tax Rules, 2026 (Notification No. 22/2026 — verify) replaced the 1962 Rules and renumbered the forms. CBDT issued FAQs on the transition (an office memorandum on s.536); read them from incometaxindia.gov.in before advising on an edge case.

## The one-axis rule: which Act governs

| Situation | Governing Act | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Income of FY 2025-26 (assessed in AY 2026-27; return filed in 2026) | **1961 Act**, old forms (ITR-1…7, Form 10-IEA for the regime option) | CBDT FAQ: "Income earned during FY 2025-26 will be governed by the Income-tax Act, 1961 and assessed in AY 2026-27" |
| Income of tax year 2026-27 onwards (1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027 and later) | **2025 Act**, 2026 Rules and renumbered forms | s.1 commencement; "tax year" in s.3 |
| Advance tax for tax year 2026-27 (instalments from June 2026) | 2025 Act (ss.403–408 per the table — verify) | the liability arises in the new tax year |
| TDS/TCS on payments made on or after 1 April 2026 | 2025 Act (ss.392–395); new forms (Form 130 etc.) | deduction event after commencement; Forms 24Q/26Q/27Q continue only for deductions up to 31 March 2026 and their corrections |
| Assessment, reassessment, penalty, appeal or prosecution **pending on 1 April 2026** | 1961 Act continues "as if the 2025 Act had not been enacted" | s.536(2) — clauses (c)–(e) and related (verify clause letters against the text) |
| Proceedings **started after 1 April 2026** for a tax year / assessment year that began before it (e.g. a s.148 notice issued in 2027 for AY 2023-24) | 1961 Act (substantive and procedural), per the savings clause and CBDT FAQ | the right/liability accrued under the old Act |
| Rights, privileges, obligations, liabilities accrued under the 1961 Act | preserved | s.536(2) read with s.6 General Clauses Act |
| Rates of tax | **Annual Finance Act**, not the Act itself (the 2025 Act carries the structure; the Finance Act 2026 amended it — verify) | unchanged practice |

A Finance Bill 2026 clause was reported to amend s.536 (itatonline digest); read the enacted text before relying on any clause number.

## Vocabulary that changed

- **Tax year** (s.3): the twelve months from 1 April; for a new business/source, from the date it comes into existence to 31 March. "Previous year" and "assessment year" are gone — but AY 2026-27 still exists under the 1961 Act for FY 2025-26 income.
- **Consolidation**: all TDS on payments other than salary sits in **s.393** as a table (serial numbers replace the 194-series letters); salary TDS is **s.392**; TCS is **s.394**; certificates for lower/nil deduction are **s.395**. Exemptions from s.10 moved into **Schedules II–VII** read with s.11; Chapter VI-A deductions became ss.122–154 (80C → 123 read with Schedule XV).
- **Fees vs penalties**: several 1961 penalties (271B, 271BA) are reported to reappear as fixed fees in s.428; s.271(1)(c) concealment penalty is reported as not carried forward (s.439 under-reporting replaces it). Verify before pleading.

## Worked examples

1. **Notice dated 15 June 2027 under "s.148" for AY 2022-23.** Old Act. The notice should cite ss.147–151A of the 1961 Act (with the 2021-amended procedure and s.148A show-cause); citing s.279–281 of the 2025 Act is wrong for that year. Flag, but note a wrong section number is usually a curable irregularity if the substance is right.
2. **TDS deducted on 10 April 2026 on a contractor payment.** New Act: s.393(1) table (serial for works contracts — verify), certificate in Form 131, statement in Form 140; the deductor's manual still saying "194C / Form 16A / 26Q" needs updating.
3. **Appeal against an AY 2024-25 assessment, filed in May 2026.** Old Act: s.246A/250 (CIT(A)) and s.253/254 (ITAT) of the 1961 Act continue; the appeal provisions of the 2025 Act apply to tax years from 2026-27.
4. **Return for FY 2025-26 filed in July 2026.** Old Act, old ITR forms, old section references (80C, 87A, 115BAC) on the form and in the computation; advising the client to claim "s.123" on it is wrong.
5. **Tax opinion on a sale of listed shares in May 2026.** New Act: s.198 (LTCG with STT) / s.196 (STCG with STT); say "corresponds to s.112A/s.111A of the 1961 Act" for the reader's orientation and check the 2025 text for any change in holding period, grandfathering or rate.

## Traps

- The same number means different things across Acts: s.10 is exemptions in 1961 but Portuguese-Civil-Code apportionment in 2025 (per one source); s.16 is salary deductions in 1961 but the definition of salary in 2025; s.80 is the loss-return rule in 1961 but a different provision in 2025.
- "Schedule" references: the 2025 Act moves exemptions and deductions into schedules with serial numbers; cite the section *and* the schedule serial.
- Business-income rows (ss.28–44) are where published concordances disagree most; read the 2025 text.
- Third-party "section finder" tools are not the Act. The department's own utility (incometaxindia.gov.in → "utility to check provisions of the Income-tax Act 1961 vis-à-vis the Income-tax Act 2025") is the only official concordance; it is a browser tool, so the user may have to open it.
