# RERA — forums, decisions and State items to check

Confirm every decision on [sci.gov.in](https://www.sci.gov.in/) (search by title; many now carry a neutral citation `YYYY INSC NNN`). Holdings are one-line summaries, not quotations.

## Forum decision table

| The allottee wants | Forum | Provision | Test / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refund of all amounts with interest (exit) | RERA **Authority** | s.18(1), s.31 | Promoter failed to complete or give possession per the agreement; allottee withdraws. Authority can order it (*Newtech*). |
| Interest for every month of delay (stay in) | RERA **Authority** | s.18(1) proviso, s.31 | Allottee does not withdraw. Interest at the State's prescribed rate until possession. |
| Possession, OC, conveyance, defect rectification, information, project directions | RERA **Authority** | ss.11, 14, 17, 19, 31, 37 | Directions and penalties; non-compliance under ss.63–64. |
| **Compensation** (loss of rent, mental agony, loss beyond interest) under ss.12, 14, 18, 19 | **Adjudicating Officer** | s.71 | Only the AO adjudges compensation; the Authority cannot (*Newtech*). 60-day disposal target. |
| Deficiency in service / unfair trade practice; buyer prefers the consumer route; compensation and refund in one place | **Consumer Commission** | s.88 RERA; CPA 2019 ss.34–35, 69 | Concurrent remedy (*Imperia*, *Experion*). 2-year limitation from cause of action. Pecuniary limits by claim value (verify current rules). |
| Promoter is insolvent; a group of buyers wants resolution | **NCLT** | s.7 IBC (allottees = financial creditors) | *Pioneer Urban*. Threshold: 100 allottees or 10% of the project's allottees, whichever is less (*Manish Kumar* upheld). |
| Criminal fraud (money diverted, fake approvals) | Police / Magistrate | BNS ss.316, 318 | Parallel track; disclose it in the RERA complaint. |

Civil courts are barred (s.79). An arbitration clause in the builder-buyer agreement does not oust RERA or the consumer forum.

## Supreme Court decisions

- **M/s Newtech Promoters and Developers Pvt Ltd v. State of Uttar Pradesh**, 11 November 2021 (2021 SCC OnLine SC 1044) — RERA applies to ongoing projects; the Authority (not only the AO) can order refund with interest under s.18; the AO's role is compensation under ss.12, 14, 18, 19; the s.43(5) pre-deposit for promoter appeals is valid; single-member benches can decide complaints.
- **Imperia Structures Ltd v. Anil Patni**, 2 November 2020 ((2020) 10 SCC 783) — RERA does not bar a consumer complaint; s.88 preserves other remedies; an allottee who booked for personal use is a consumer.
- **Experion Developers Pvt Ltd v. Sushma Ashok Shiroor**, 7 April 2022 (2022 SCC OnLine SC 416) — consumer commissions may order refund with interest for delay; concurrent jurisdiction confirmed; interest as compensation.
- **Pioneer Urban Land and Infrastructure Ltd v. Union of India**, 9 August 2019 ((2019) 8 SCC 416) — allottees are financial creditors under the IBC; RERA, CPA and IBC remedies coexist.
- **Manish Kumar v. Union of India**, 19 January 2021 ((2021) 5 SCC 1) — the 100-allottee / 10% threshold for allottee-initiated insolvency is valid.
- **Pioneer Urban Land and Infrastructure Ltd v. Govindan Raghavan**, 2 April 2019 ((2019) 5 SCC 725) — one-sided builder-buyer clauses (token delay compensation vs. steep buyer default interest) are unfair and do not bind the buyer.
- **Ireo Grace Realtech Pvt Ltd v. Abhishek Khanna**, 11 January 2021 ((2021) 3 SCC 241) — a buyer cannot be made to wait indefinitely; one-sided terms are unenforceable; buyers who got OC-backed offers of possession before filing were treated differently from those who had not.
- **Wg Cdr Arifur Rahman Khan v. DLF Southern Homes Pvt Ltd**, 24 August 2020 ((2020) 16 SCC 512) — buyers who took possession can still claim delay compensation beyond a token contractual clause.
- **Forum for People's Collective Efforts v. State of West Bengal**, 4 May 2021 ((2021) 8 SCC 599) — West Bengal's parallel HIRA struck down as repugnant to RERA; RERA occupies the field.
- **Supertech Ltd v. Emerald Court Owner Resident Welfare Association**, 31 August 2021 ((2021) 10 SCC 1) — illegal construction in breach of sanctioned plans; refund with interest to buyers of the demolished towers (context for s.14 "no alteration without consent").

## State items to look up every time

| Item | Where | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Prescribed interest rate | State RERA Rules (e.g. Rule 15 UP Rules; Rule 18 Maharashtra Rules) — usually "SBI highest MCLR + 2%"; some States use a different benchmark | Drives every rupee figure; check SBI's current MCLR on sbi.co.in on the date of calculation |
| Complaint form and fee | State portal (Form M/N/"Complaint" and fee rule; commonly ₹1,000–₹5,000 — verify) | Portal rejects wrong form/fee |
| Project registration page | State portal project search | Registration number, declared completion date, extensions, quarterly updates, litigation disclosures |
| COVID / force-majeure extension orders | State Authority's "Orders/Circulars" page (2020–21 orders typically extended completion dates by 6–9 months) | Delay runs from the extended date, not the agreement date |
| Model agreement for sale | State Rules annexure | Clauses that deviate from the model are a point against the promoter |
| Conciliation forum | Some States (e.g. MahaRERA) run a conciliation forum before adjudication | Optional faster route; record consent carefully |
| Appellate Tribunal | State REAT (some States share one) | Filing rules and pre-deposit practice |
