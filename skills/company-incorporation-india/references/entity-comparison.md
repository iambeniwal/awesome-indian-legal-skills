# Entity comparison and post-incorporation checklist

Verify every section and figure on [India Code](https://www.indiacode.gov.in/) and [mca.gov.in](https://www.mca.gov.in/) before relying on it; record the date checked.

## Which form

| | Private limited | OPC | Section 8 company | LLP | Partnership firm | Sole proprietorship |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Law | Companies Act 2013, s.2(68) | s.2(62); Rules 3–7 | s.8 + Rule 19–22 | LLP Act 2008 | Indian Partnership Act 1932 | none (registrations only) |
| Minimum people | 2 members, 2 directors | 1 member + 1 nominee, 1 director | 2 members, 2 directors | 2 partners (both designated) | 2 partners | 1 |
| Resident requirement | one director ≥182 days in FY (s.149(3)) | member and nominee must be Indian citizens; member resident (120 days, verify) | as private company | one designated partner resident | none | none |
| Liability | limited to shares | limited | limited | limited to contribution | unlimited, joint and several | unlimited |
| Minimum capital | none (fee nil up to ₹15 lakh authorised) | none | none | none (contribution any amount) | none | none |
| Governance | board, ≥4 meetings/yr (small company: 2), AGM | board, 2 meetings/yr, no AGM | board + AGM; licence conditions | LLP Agreement; no board/AGM | deed | — |
| Annual MCA filings | AOC-4, MGT-7/7A, ADT-1, DIR-3 KYC, DPT-3, MSME-1 | AOC-4 (180 days), MGT-7A, DIR-3 KYC | as private + CSR/licence conditions | Form 11, Form 8, DIR-3 KYC | none (Registrar of Firms is State-level) | none |
| Audit | mandatory | mandatory | mandatory | only if turnover >₹40 lakh or contribution >₹25 lakh | tax audit thresholds only | tax audit thresholds only |
| Outside equity / ESOP | yes — the investor default | no (single member; convert first) | no dividends; grants/CSR-eligible | no shares; investors rarely accept | no | no |
| Foreign investment | automatic route in most sectors; FC-GPR | not permitted for non-citizens | permitted with FCRA care for donations | FDI permitted in automatic-route sectors (verify) | NRI on non-repatriation basis only (verify) | — |
| Conversion paths | to public/OPC/LLP (conditions) | to private company (voluntary; mandatory thresholds removed 2021 — verify) | restricted | to private company (2022 Rules) | to LLP/company | to any (fresh incorporation) |
| Typical use | startups, anything raising money | solo founder wanting limited liability | NGOs, foundations, clubs | professionals, services, family businesses | small trading, informal | freelancers, small shops |

Section 8: cannot use "Private Limited"/"Limited" in the name; licence can be revoked for breaching objects; surplus cannot be distributed.

## Incorporation documents (company)

- For each subscriber/director: PAN, Aadhaar/passport, address proof ≤2 months (bank statement/utility bill), photograph, mobile and email (OTP-verified on V3), DSC class 3; foreign nationals — passport and address proof apostilled/notarised, and the Press Note 3 (2020) security-clearance check for land-border countries.
- Registered office: ownership proof or rent agreement + NOC from the owner + utility bill ≤2 months; or file INC-22 within 30 days (s.12).
- Objects clause (main objects, matters ancillary) for e-MoA; capital clause; article choices for e-AoA (entrenchment, share transfer restrictions — a private company must restrict transfers, s.2(68)).
- DIR-2 consents, INC-9 declaration (auto), professional's declaration in INC-8/14 as applicable, nominee consent INC-3 for OPC, INC-12 projections for Section 8.
- Trademark search printout for the name (good practice; MCA checks for conflicts).

## DPIIT Startup recognition (as of the skill's version date — verify)

Private company, LLP or registered partnership; ≤10 years from incorporation; turnover ≤₹100 crore in any FY; working towards innovation/improvement or a scalable model with employment/wealth potential; not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business. Benefits: self-certification under some labour/environment laws, s.80-IAC tax holiday on IMB approval (now under the Income-tax Act 2025 — verify the corresponding section), angel-tax relief history, faster IP processing, public-procurement relaxations.
